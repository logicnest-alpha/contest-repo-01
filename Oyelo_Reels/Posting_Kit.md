## Make the CTA actually work
- **Reply to every "DEMO" comment within a few hours**, with a DM: "Hi! Which shop do you run? I can show you Oyelo on your own menu/QR in 10 minutes, at your shop or on a call."
- **Oyelo's own WhatsApp number isn't live yet.** Once it is, change the CTA to "WhatsApp DEMO to [number]". For local owners, that converts better than comments.
- Put **oyelo.in** in your bio link.
- **Walk-in follow-up:** show the relevant reel on your phone when you visit shops in person. It's a 30-second pitch.

## Local reach tips
- Add the location tag **Warangal** / **Hanamkonda** to every post.
- Consider a **Telugu voice version** of the best 2–3 reels. Local owners respond to their language.
  (The current voice is English and synthetic. Record your own voice in Telugu or English for best results.)

## Honesty guardrails (keep these)
- No made-up numbers or results in captions. When a real shop gets real reviews, make a new reel with their permission.
- Never suggest rewarding reviews or only asking happy customers. Oyelo's pitch is that it stays within Google's rules.
