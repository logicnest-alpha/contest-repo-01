## Caption (Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts)

Your first clients come from conversations, not from posting more. Here's the system I'm testing:

1️⃣ One customer + one painful problem
Not "I build websites" → "I help dentists fill empty appointment slots"

2️⃣ Write down 100 people with that problem
Start with people you know, then ask "who do you know who…?"

3️⃣ Don't pitch. Show.
A 2-minute video with 2 real fixes on THEIR page, for free

4️⃣ On the call, ask more than you talk (70/30)
"What is this costing you?"
"What have you tried?"
"What happens if nothing changes?"

5️⃣ Offer a small paid pilot
One outcome · one price · 2 weeks. Low risk for them, proof for you

6️⃣ Deliver → ask for a testimonial → ask "who else?"

Then repeat: 10 conversations a day.

I'm still learning this. Clients don't buy effort, they buy a problem solved by someone they trust.
